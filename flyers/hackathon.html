What's the IRW? The Item Response Warehouse is a free, open collection of harmonized item response data: more than 8,600 datasets and over 6.6 billion responses from education, psychology and beyond, ready to pull straight into R or Python.

Two tracks

Track 1

Academic

Write a short, original research paper using IRW's vast reservoir of psychometric data.

Leave with a paper, working code, and the opportunity to co-author with GSE faculty.

Track 2

Entrepreneurial

Build an open-science project, a site, or a startup using IRW's data architecture for any field beyond education.

Walk out with a new MVP and the opportunity to demo to a panel of investors and VC-backed entrepreneurs.

Get ready

  • Getting started: install the R or Python package and pull your first table.
  • Browse the data: filter the warehouse by size, construct, field and response type.
  • The core standard: the one format every table shares, and the pattern the entrepreneurial track can borrow for other fields.
  • Using an AI assistant: point your assistant at the warehouse.
  • Vignettes: worked analyses across many datasets at once.
