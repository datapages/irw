Pilot — not the full warehouse

Only 25 of the IRW's 4,134 tables have a page. These 25 were chosen to test the page generator against the corpus' awkward cases — the largest and smallest tables, tables with and without item text, tagged and untagged, and names that break naive URL handling. They are not the most important tables, and the selection is not a recommendation.

To search the whole warehouse, use Browse the IRW Data. Whether this expands to every table is being decided in irw#1706.
